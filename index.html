Vehicles enter under SUMO control for about three seconds; then the model drives. It outputs longitudinal acceleration only and follows its SUMO route. When a vehicle reaches the end of its route, a fixed rule drives it out.

Development scenes only; the sealed test set has not been used. Positions between whole seconds are interpolated for display.

Model: Traffic Surrogate Model, seed 2, training update 6000. Ego-centred bird's-eye input (49 × 49 cells of 3 m), convolutional U-Net, about 2 M parameters. Scene: fixed-time signal, 58 s cycle, single lane per direction. Video version (mp4, 94 s).
